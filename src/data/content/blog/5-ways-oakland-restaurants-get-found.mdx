If you run a restaurant in Oakland, you already know the competition is fierce. Between delivery apps, national chains, and the spot that just opened down the street, getting noticed feels harder every year.

But some Oakland restaurants are beating the odds. Here are five things they're doing differently to get found by the people who live and work on their block.

## 1. They show up when neighbors search

When someone in Temescal types "best tacos near me" into their phone, certain restaurants appear first. That doesn't happen by accident. These restaurants have made sure their online presence matches what people are actually searching for — their menu, their hours, their neighborhood, and real photos of their food.

## 2. They make it easy to choose them

Getting found is only half the battle. The restaurants pulling in the most new customers make it dead simple to take the next step. Their phone number is front and center. Their menu loads fast. Their reviews are recent and real. There's no guessing involved.

## 3. They lean into their neighborhood

Oakland isn't one place — it's dozens of neighborhoods, each with its own personality. The restaurants that thrive in Fruitvale talk about Fruitvale. The ones in Jack London Square reference the waterfront. They don't try to be everything to everyone. They're proud of their block, and it shows.

## 4. They ask happy customers to spread the word

The most effective marketing for a restaurant is still word of mouth — it's just that "word of mouth" now includes online reviews, social media posts, and text messages. Restaurants that actively (and politely) ask satisfied customers to leave a review see a real difference in how many new faces walk through the door.

## 5. They stop wasting money on what doesn't work

Too many restaurant owners pour money into flashy ads or social media campaigns that don't bring anyone in. The Oakland restaurants that are growing have gotten honest about what's actually working and doubled down on that. Sometimes it's as simple as making sure their Google listing is accurate and up to date.

## The bottom line

You don't need a massive budget to get found. You need to show up where your neighbors are looking, make it easy for them to choose you, and give them a reason to come back.
